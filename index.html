What is ProData AI?

ProData AI is an automated data scientist tool built with Python and Streamlit. Upload a CSV or Excel file and automatically receive machine learning model comparisons, time series forecasts, business driver analysis, AI-powered insights, an interactive dashboard, and a professional PDF report — in approximately 30 seconds.

Who is ProData AI for?

Data analysts, freelancers, business owners, consultants, and small teams who need professional data science outputs without hiring a full-time data scientist or paying expensive consulting fees.

How does the AutoML engine work?

ProData AI simultaneously trains six ML models — Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Ridge, Lasso, Decision Tree, and KNN. All are evaluated and ranked on a leaderboard by R² score for regression tasks or accuracy for classification tasks.

Does ProData AI detect anomalies?

Yes. ProData AI automatically flags outliers and irregular data points using statistical and ML-based anomaly detection, ranking each one so unusual spikes, errors, or fraud patterns are easy to spot.
